# Saftladen Weihnachtsfilm: script draft v1

Auto-play, German subtitles, ~4 min, silent (own playlist). Every team member gets a beat.
`[?]` = I know nothing, you fill. `[check]` = joke touches a person, please vet before the dinner.

Rules of the film
- Time is pinned by the director per scene (night / morning / midday), Christmas mode on, snow on.
- Presence caps (max 15 office, 8 home) are overridden by the director: cast is placed per scene, not random.
- Camera = hand-written shots (x, z, azimuth, polar, distance) with eased flights, like the Aktionen camera.
- Subtitle = lower third, big, letterbox bars, one line, ~3-5 s each.
